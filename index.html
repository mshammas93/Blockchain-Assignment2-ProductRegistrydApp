Product Registry dApp

MG3012 Blockchain Technology for Business: Assignment 2

About this dApp

This app lets small businesses list products on the Ethereum Sepolia testnet. Every product is stored in a smart contract, so the records are public and can't be changed by anyone except the seller who listed them.

How to use it

  1. Click Connect MetaMask and approve the connection.
  2. Add a product with a name and a price (PKR). MetaMask will ask you to confirm the transaction.
  3. Wait for the transaction to be confirmed, then see it in the product list below.
  4. You can update the price of any product that you listed.

Useful links: View contract on Etherscan · MetaMask docs · ethers.js docs
